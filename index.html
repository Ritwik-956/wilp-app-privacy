Privacy Policy – WILP App

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the WILP Mentor–Mentee mobile application (“the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects information when you use it. The App is intended for students, mentors and staff of the BITS Pilani Work Integrated Learning Programmes (WILP) to run mentorship activities.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, role (mentor or mentee) and login credentials. If you sign in with institutional SSO or Google, we receive your name and email address from that provider.
  • Profile information: details you add to your profile, such as a profile photo, bio, programme and professional details.
  • Mentorship content: sessions you schedule or join, session feedback, goals, notes, tasks and subtasks, and files or links you submit.
  • Messages: chat messages, images and files you exchange with other users in the App.
  • Photos: only the images you choose from your photo library (for a profile picture or to share in chat). We do not access your library otherwise.
  • Device information: a push notification token and basic device details needed to deliver notifications.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or use your camera or microphone. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To provide mentorship features: sessions, tasks, goals, feedback and chat.
  • To send notifications about messages, upcoming sessions and task deadlines.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the service.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Sharing of information

  • Other users: your profile, messages and submissions are visible to the mentors or mentees you interact with.
  • Programme administrators: authorised WILP staff may access mentorship records for programme administration.
  • Service providers: we use hosting, cloud file storage and push notification services (such as Amazon Web Services and Expo / Apple Push Notification service) that process data on our behalf.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

4. Data storage and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Login tokens are stored on your device using the operating system’s secure storage. Files you upload are stored on secured cloud storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed for programme administration. When an account is deleted, associated personal data is deleted or anonymised unless we are required to keep it by law.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and update your profile information in the App.
  • You can turn off notifications or photo access at any time in your device settings.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and personal data by contacting us at the address below.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for enrolled students and staff and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
